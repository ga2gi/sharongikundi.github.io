01 — ABOUT

Working where people, policy and technology meet.

I am a governance and public policy professional from Kenya passionate about creating meaningful pathways for young people to participate in governance and policy processes.

I am the Founder and Director of PolicyBridge KE , a youth-led public policy and civic engagement organization focused on connecting grassroots perspectives with actionable policy ideas.

My work and interests span youth engagement, participatory governance, civic technology, digital governance, responsible AI, data and sustainable development.

I am particularly interested in how technology can make institutions more accessible, participation more meaningful and policy more responsive to the people it affects.
